# Credits

ArenaMP exists because of the work of the OpenMW and TES3MP communities.

- OpenMW created the open-source Morrowind engine foundation. The historical contributor list is retained in [AUTHORS.md](AUTHORS.md).
- TES3MP added multiplayer networking, synchronization, server-side Lua, the dedicated server, browser, and master-server foundations. The original detailed credits are retained in [docs/upstream/TES3MP_CREDITS.md](docs/upstream/TES3MP_CREDITS.md).
- ArenaMP contributors extended that foundation with the systems documented in [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md).
- EncoreMP companion content is distributed under `extras/encoremp/` with its original documentation.
- OpenMoji artwork is used in the optional color emoji atlas under CC BY-SA 4.0; see [THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md).

Upstream projects:

- [OpenMW](https://gitlab.com/OpenMW/openmw)
- [TES3MP](https://github.com/TES3MP/TES3MP)
- [OpenMoji](https://openmoji.org/)
